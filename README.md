# Water management trade-offs in Lomellina rice paddies

## Final course report

[Read the Ecosystem Functioning and Services report](reports/efs/Lomellina_EFS_Final_Report.pdf)

This University of Milan course report examines how rice-paddy water management affects greenhouse-gas emissions, irrigation and connected hydrology, rice production, and aquatic biodiversity in Lomellina, Pavia. It is a targeted critical review with spatial context and secondary analysis. The report distinguishes local field evidence, irrigation-district modelling, and evidence from other regions.

## Continuing research

The repository also contains ongoing Sentinel-1 and groundwater analyses. Date-matched MEDWATERICE field observations are available for selected 2020 satellite acquisitions, but plot-level spatial matching and water status at the acquisition time have not yet been verified. These observations do not currently validate a SAR flood-duration measure or establish a causal groundwater effect.

## Evidence and attribution

The report identifies its literature, data sources, methods, and limitations. Findings from cited studies remain attributable to their original authors. This course report is not a peer-reviewed publication.
